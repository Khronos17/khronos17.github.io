# Team Daily Radio

Team Daily Radio is a personal, non-commercial tool. Every day it builds one YouTube playlist of about an hour of music for a small team of colleagues, based on the genres and moods they ask for.

It uses the YouTube Data API only to search for music videos and to create and update playlists on the operator's own YouTube channel. It does not sign in other people and does not collect their data.
